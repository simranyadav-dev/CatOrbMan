How to Play

Game Controls:

  • 🎮 Use Arrow Keys or WASD to move your cat
  • 🟡 Collect all the dots to advance to the next level
  • ⚡ Power pellets make ghosts vulnerable for a short time
  • 👻 Avoid ghosts or you'll lose a life
  • 🏆 Score points by eating dots and ghosts

Scoring:

  • 🔸 Small dot: 10 points
  • 🔶 Power pellet: 50 points
  • 👻 Ghost (when vulnerable): 200 points
